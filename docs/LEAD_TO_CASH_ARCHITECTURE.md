# GODS COMMAND AI — Lead-to-Cash Architecture (V1.1.0 & V1.2.0)

## Overview
GODS COMMAND AI is an enterprise-grade AI operating system and revenue engine for home-service contractors (HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, etc.).

## Complete Workflow Journey
1. **Customer Inquiry Capture**: Multi-channel intake (web form, mobile, API, Twilio voice webhook).
2. **AI Lead Qualification Agent**: Analyzes urgency, intent, problem summary, and confidence score.
3. **Estimate Generation**: Uses approved service catalog and decimal-safe arithmetic.
4. **Owner Approval & Customer Portal**: Secure tokenized link for customer review and explicit acceptance.
5. **Automatic Job Creation**: Transactionally converts accepted estimates into scheduled field jobs.
6. **Scheduling & Dispatch**: Technician assignment and route optimization.
7. **Job Completion & Invoicing**: Technicians record labor/materials; invoices generate automatically.
8. **Payment Collection & Verification**: Stripe secure checkout with signed webhook reconciliation.
9. **Job Profitability Engine**: Calculates gross job profit, margins, and realized cash flow.
10. **Revenue Recovery (V1.2)**: Missed-call SMS recovery, intelligent estimate follow-up sequences, and recurring maintenance contracts.
